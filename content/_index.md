Hello. My name is Colan Biemer (he/him). Fun fact about my name: It was misspelled on the birth certificate. So for the pronunciation of my name, imagine my name was spelled like "Colin."

I am a lecturer at [Bentley University](https://www.bentley.edu/). Before that, I did my PhD at Northeastern University, where I was advised by Seth Cooper. My [dissertation](./pdf/2025_colan_biemer_dissertation.pdf) explored using reinforcement learning to guide procedural content generation for dynamic difficulty adjustment in games.

When I'm not working o, I'm likely doing something with my perfect dog or reading a book. My two favorite books are _A Christmas Carol_ by Charles Dickens and _Stoner_ by John Williams.
